Food, and the four buildings that make it

Soldiers should march on bread and bacon, not on raw wheat. That splits one good into a chain, and the chain needs four buildings the free KayKit Medieval Hexagon pack was never going to hand us whole. What this does to the barracks

Wheat stops being a war material and goes back to being a crop: it feeds the mill and the animals, and nothing else. Food is what a soldier costs, which means an army needs a working food chain behind it rather than one farm — and it gives the fishery a reason to exist on a coastal map where the wheat belt is somebody else’s.

Unit Costs today Costs after
Knight 3 wheat · 1 sword 3 food · 1 sword
Spearman 2 wheat · 1 spear 2 food · 1 spear
Archer 2 wheat · 1 bow 2 food · 1 bow
Brewery 1 wheat · 1 water unchanged — ale is still brewed from the grain

Half of this is now built. Flour and food are goods, the windmill grinds wheat, a bakery turns flour and water into bread, and the barracks trains on bread — the ledger above is live. The bakery went in as the bake-house (the pack’s second house with a hand-built clay oven bolted to its flank) because that needed no new machinery in the renderer; the oven house is still the better look and is a contained swap when you want it. The fishery and the livestock pen are the two the EXTRA pack changes most, so they are waiting.

Tell me which model wins each section and the swap is a line or two — the compositions here are already the code that would ship.

And if you drop the EXTRA pack’s Assets/gltf folder into public/models/kaykit, say so: its pier, boats, shipyard and herd delete most of the hand-built column above, and every card here is one line of a recipe list, so re-cutting them against the real models is quick.
